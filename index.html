Brand & Community Impact

HERC GTH is an independent hip-hop artist, entrepreneur, and community figure from Garland, Texas, widely recognized by his supporters and local media as "The King of Garland."[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span)


The acronym GTH stands for God, Truth, Health, serving as the foundation for his brand, music label, and holistic lifestyle movement focused on progressing spiritually, mentally, and physically[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
